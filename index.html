        Confirm it with a dermatologist

        Your scan is a starting point. In a 20-minute Skin Check, a dermatologist assesses your skin type, main concerns and how humidity affects your skin. Olive Young advisors use the results to fine-tune your routine, and you take home a free trial bundle.

        How the scan works

        A face-mapping model (MediaPipe Face Landmarker) places 478 points on your face in this browser. The scan resizes every face to the same scale, then reads five zones in CIELAB colour: specular highlights (shine), red balance and red spots (redness), fine luminance detail (texture) and mid-scale brightness variation and dark spots (tone). It won't give readings if the face is too small, turned, blurry, badly exposed or lit from one side, and live scans must agree across 5 frames. Scores are relative indicators, not clinical measurements. Light and camera still affect them. Not a medical or diagnostic tool.

        About the products
